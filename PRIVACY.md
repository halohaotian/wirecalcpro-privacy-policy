# Privacy Policy for WireCalc Pro

**Last updated: May 28, 2026**

TenxPro ("we", "our", or "us") operates the WireCalc Pro mobile application. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our application.

## Information We Collect

WireCalc Pro is a professional wire gauge calculator app. **We do not collect any personal data or user information.**

Our application works entirely offline. All calculations (wire gauge, voltage drop, conduit fill) are performed locally on your device. No data is transmitted to our servers or any third parties.

## How We Use Information

Since we collect no data, we do not use any personal information.

## Data Storage

All your inputs and calculation results are stored locally on your device. We do not maintain any external databases or cloud storage for user data.

## Third-Party Services

WireCalc Pro does not integrate with any third-party services, analytics platforms, or advertising networks.
