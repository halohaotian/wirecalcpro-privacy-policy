Privacy Policy for WireCalc Pro

Last updated: May 28, 2026

Introduction

WireCalc Pro ("the App") is developed by Jianhui Wu ("the Developer"). This privacy policy explains how the App handles your information.

Data Collection

WireCalc Pro does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

The App is a standalone professional wire sizing and electrical calculation tool. All calculations are performed entirely on your device. No data is sent to any server, cloud service, or third party.

Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone number)
  • Device identifiers or advertising identifiers
  • Location data
  • Calculation inputs or results
  • Usage analytics or behavioral data
  • Crash logs or performance data
  • Any other user data of any kind

No Third-Party Services

WireCalc Pro does not integrate with or use any third-party services, including but not limited to:

  • Analytics platforms (e.g., Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics)
  • Advertising networks or SDKs
  • Social media SDKs
  • Crash reporting tools (e.g., Crashlytics, Sentry)
  • Cloud storage or synchronization services
  • Payment or in-app purchase frameworks

Data Storage

All calculation values you enter into the App exist only temporarily in the device memory during use. When you close the App, all entered values are cleared. The App does not persistently store any user-entered data.

No Internet Access Required

WireCalc Pro functions entirely offline. All wire gauge data, NEC (National Electrical Code) reference tables, and calculation formulas are built into the App. No internet connection is required to use any feature of the App.

App Features and Data Handling

The App provides the following calculation tools, all of which operate entirely on-device with no external data transmission:

  • Wire Gauge Calculator — Finds AWG wire size based on current (amps) and temperature rating (60C, 75C, 90C) using NEC Table 310.16 data
  • Voltage Drop Calculator — Calculates voltage drop over distance for single-phase and three-phase systems, supporting both copper and aluminum conductors
  • Conduit Fill Calculator — Determines conduit fill percentage for EMT, PVC Schedule 40, and PVC Schedule 80 conduit types per NEC Chapter 9 Table 4
  • Cost Estimator — Estimates material costs including configurable waste factor

Permissions

WireCalc Pro does not request any device permissions. The App does not access:

  • Camera or microphone
  • Photo library
  • Location services
  • Contacts
  • Files or storage
  • Network or internet

Children's Privacy

WireCalc Pro is suitable for users of all ages. Since no data is collected, there are no specific concerns regarding children's data. The App is rated 4+ on the App Store.

Changes to This Policy

If the Developer makes changes to the App that affect data handling practices, this privacy policy will be updated accordingly. Any material changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
